Nimbus, the cloud in the corner, reads the news so you don't have to, and rates it on the [Beaufort scale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beaufort_scale), which sailors have used to describe wind since 1805 and which was never meant for this. He's moody. Clicking him helps.

One reading a day. When several things happen at once, the worst weather wins: a storm beats a release-day snow, and snow beats sunshine. Release days snow whatever got released.
